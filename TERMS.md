## Not a medical device

WLBC displays and summarizes measurements produced by consumer devices. It is not
a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
Body-composition figures from consumer bioimpedance scales are estimates, and can
be affected by hydration, food, and time of day. Do not make medical decisions
based on this tool. Talk to a qualified professional about your health.
